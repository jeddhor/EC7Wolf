Thank you for installing EC7Wolf, a port of Corridor 7: Alien Invasion.

EC7Wolf does not include the game itself. You need the data files from your own
copy of Corridor 7: the ones named *.CO7, and CORR7CD.EXE, which is needed
too because Corridor 7 keeps its palette inside its own executable.

Put them on your phone as a folder or a zip file, then press IMPORT GAME DATA on
the PLAY tab and point it at them. The files are copied into this app's own
storage, so you only have to do it once.

If the CD animations (SEQONE.CO7, SEQTHREE.CO7, SEQFOUR.CO7) or a ripped
soundtrack (track03.ogg and friends) are alongside them, those are imported too.

The Android launcher is based on the work of Beloko Games.
