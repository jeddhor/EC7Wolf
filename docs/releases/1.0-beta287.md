# EC7Wolf 1.0-beta287

Prerelease, 9 November 2026. Based on ECWolf 1.4.2-9-g1bff92d
(18 February 2026), on upstream's 1.5.0pre line.

This beta includes the verified issue corrections and approved optional HUD
and gameplay enhancements from [PR #13](https://github.com/jeddhor/EC7Wolf/pull/13).
You need your own Corridor 7: Alien Invasion CD-release data. Public downloads
contain the port, installer and editor; they do not contain commercial game data.

## Corrections

- [#1](https://github.com/jeddhor/EC7Wolf/issues/1): Fast mouse sweeps retain
  their displacement and fractional sensitivity instead of losing turning at
  the shared movement clamp.
- [#3](https://github.com/jeddhor/EC7Wolf/issues/3): Open animated elevator
  apertures permit shots, projectiles and alien traversal; closed apertures
  and solid glass retain blocking.
- [#6](https://github.com/jeddhor/EC7Wolf/issues/6): The fan wall supplies its
  original single-use visor battery, adds 100 charge even above 100, and changes
  to spent artwork. Older save triggers and the editor prefab are corrected.
- [#7](https://github.com/jeddhor/EC7Wolf/issues/7): Moving probes use the
  original movement frames and timing instead of inserting the raised idle pose.
- [#8](https://github.com/jeddhor/EC7Wolf/issues/8): Red-access and floor-plan
  inventory icons use their correct original graphics.
- [#9](https://github.com/jeddhor/EC7Wolf/issues/9): Corporal and Lieutenant
  receive a floor plan on each campaign floor, including travel and load paths.
- [#10](https://github.com/jeddhor/EC7Wolf/issues/10): All four original weapon
  ready poses are available in both renderers, restoring vertical motion where
  it is present in the artwork and retaining the shotgun's nearly level poses.
- [#11](https://github.com/jeddhor/EC7Wolf/issues/11): Red and blue ceiling lamps
  are decorations, not collectible access cards. Older saved world actors and
  editor descriptions are repaired. Existing owned access is preserved because
  old saves cannot distinguish legitimate terminal grants from the former bug.

## Optional enhancements

- [#5](https://github.com/jeddhor/EC7Wolf/issues/5): **Options > Graphics >
  Full-screen Mini HUD** supplies compact status at 100% port view size. It
  is a port enhancement: we could not identify a dedicated original mini-HUD
  asset/layout or reproduce the reported mode in original-game playtesting.
  The largest original view observed by Jason retained the normal status bar.
  Holding Show Status displays the full bar in software and OpenGL.
- [#12](https://github.com/jeddhor/EC7Wolf/issues/12): **Numeric Status Bar**
  independently replaces the large bar's health, armor and ammunition meters
  with exact totals, including over-max values. It defaults off.
- **Options > Gameplay > Shotgun Minimum 50** applies an optional 50-point
  floor after distance calculation. It defaults off; classic damage, firing
  rate and the upper damage formula are retained. The host decides in
  multiplayer, saves restore their own rule, and older saves/legacy demos use
  classic damage. The current mission's effective rule appears in the menu.

Multiplayer uses protocol 8. All peers must use a matching build.

## Validation and remaining work

Original-game evidence and its limits are recorded in
[the verification report](../github-issue-verification-2026-11-08.md).
Focused runtime tests cover the corrected behaviors, old-save migration,
menu persistence, actual HUD content in software and live OpenGL, and host/client
rule agreement. The runnable local package is rebuilt and checked through the
normal launcher. Release automation builds new platform packages and installers
without commercial data.

- [#4 — audio balance](https://github.com/jeddhor/EC7Wolf/issues/4) remains in
  progress pending a matched original-game comparison. No music/effects gain
  adjustment is included.
- [#14 — alien counter](https://github.com/jeddhor/EC7Wolf/issues/14) is a
  separate open report affecting later floors; it is not fixed in this beta.
- The quoted shotgun strategy-guide range remains unverified. The optional
  floor is an approved balance choice, not a claim of restored original damage.
