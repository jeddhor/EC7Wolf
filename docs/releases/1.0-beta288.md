# EC7Wolf 1.0-beta288

Prerelease, 9 November 2026. Based on ECWolf 1.4.2-9-g1bff92d
(18 February 2026), on upstream's 1.5.0pre line.

Fixes [#14](https://github.com/jeddhor/EC7Wolf/issues/14): M-343 and Assault
Cannon bursts could credit the same alien death repeatedly, causing early
elevator clearance and kill percentages above 100%. Dead aliens now stop
receiving damage, preventing duplicate kills, points awards, and drop rolls.
Disguised Bandors were already included in the starting alien population.

Affected single-player saves repair their current-floor kill counter when
the saved actor population accounts for the full total. Premature clearance
notifications are reset. Historical excess score, extra pickups, and completed
floor statistics are preserved because they cannot be reconstructed reliably.

Original-game playtests confirmed one kill credit for lethal M-343 and Assault
Cannon attacks. Focused regressions cover both weapons, every Bandor disguise,
ordinary aliens, save repair, elevator clearance, and Level 5/6 populations.
See [verification details and limits](../github-issue14-verification-2026-11-09.md).

Multiplayer uses **protocol 9**; all peers must update to a matching build.
This prevents older clients from joining with the faulty burst behavior.

Fresh platform packages and installers accompany this beta. You need your own
Corridor 7 CD-release data; public downloads contain no commercial game data.
[#4 — audio balance](https://github.com/jeddhor/EC7Wolf/issues/4) remains in
progress pending the original-game comparison. No audio balance adjustment is
included.
