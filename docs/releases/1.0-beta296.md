# EC7Wolf 1.0-beta296

EC7Wolf's beta number counts commits since milestone `20ee748`; it is separate
from its engine base, ECWolf 1.4.2-9-g1bff92d (18 February 2026), on upstream's
1.5.0pre line. This remains a prerelease beta.

## Original-game fidelity

- **Ammo-capacity packs and duplicate floor plans (#16):** packs add 500 to
  bullet capacity, up to 999, without adding ammunition. They can be collected
  at full ammunition or maximum capacity. Redundant floor plans also collect.
  DOS tests confirmed capacity 200 → 700 → 999, unchanged ammunition, and a
  further pack consumed at the cap.
- **Inset map (#17):** living aliens flash yellow, placed mines flash red,
  and the floor plan reveals stationary/disguised aliens. Closed doors no
  longer hide mapped rooms. The native 24×24 sampling window is restored.
  Floor code 259 fills hidden maze passages gray while retaining their wall
  outlines; all 351 relevant MAP04 floor cells matched the DOS bitmap.
- **Adrenaline (#18):** health above 100 returns to 100 when entering another
  campaign floor. Wounded players keep their existing health, and same-floor
  save/load retains temporary boosted health.
- **Rank-specific alien health (#19):** use all five original species-specific
  health rows, including President, independently of enemy spawn eligibility.
  Existing saved aliens retain their current health. No unsupported blanket
  health or damage multiplier is introduced.
- **Glass rendering (#21):** restore the missing perpendicular face at native
  glass corners and match the original first-glass-cell visibility rule in
  software and OpenGL. A controlled two-pane DOS test confirmed that a nearer
  pane also hides the opaque border of a farther pane. Sprite visibility in
  front of and behind glass remains correct, including filtered/MSAA rendering.
- **Pickup lighting (#22):** supply/weapon pickups retain full brightness in
  dark areas, using their existing animation frames. Scenery, disguised aliens
  and deployed mines are not blanket-marked full-bright.
- **Wall indicators and first-person lighting:** retain the original yellow
  ready/red empty dispenser-light colors at distance and under infrared, while
  shading their housings normally. Weapons and hands use native source
  brightness, with visor palettes and weapon color cycling still applied.

## HUD and optional shotgun settings

- **Classic compact HUD (#5, #12, #20):** select Classic or Modern. The original
  compact HUD was reproduced in DOS after providing enough conventional memory
  for view size 21. Classic uses the native font, gauges, icons and readouts.
- Unfilled compact-meter areas are transparent. Corrected fill height and
  bevel corners match the DOS meters; the last numeral row sits inside the
  colored well. Full-screen weapon scale and resting position match DOS.
- Turning the mini HUD off immediately restores the full status bar and normal
  viewport. Turning it on restores the compact full-screen view at maximum
  size; deliberately smaller views keep their selected size. The choice
  persists, including when upgrading an old HUD-off/view-21 configuration.
- **Mini HUD Score** hides the compact score/frags field independently, for
  either style. It defaults on, as in a fresh DOS game; DOS's S key toggles
  that field. In the port, use Options → Graphics → Mini HUD Score.
- Numeric Status Bar covers health, armor, ammunition, mines and visor charge,
  with readable numbers over colored gauges, including over-maximum totals.
- Shotgun Damage offers Classic, Minimum 50 and Minimum 100. Classic retains
  the verified original distance-dependent damage. The minimum is optional,
  applies to new games, is stored in saves and is chosen by the multiplayer
  host. Earlier enhanced settings/saves retain Minimum 50.

## Compatibility and validation

Multiplayer peers must use the same beta: the shotgun rule uses network
protocol 10. Existing saves retain their serialized alien health, inventory
and pickup state; the release does not recreate items already consumed in an
older build. Existing save product-version compatibility is preserved.

Verified against the locally owned, unmodified retail DOS executable through
controlled playtests and memory inspection. Local checks include eight weapon
silhouettes, compact/full-HUD switching and persistence, score visibility,
transparent meter interiors, native and widescreen/1920×1080 presentation,
native wall-light colors in normal/infrared views, glass/sprite depth behavior,
map bitmap comparisons, campaign pickups/travel/rank rules, save/network rule
compatibility, retail software/OpenGL parity, filtering/palette/MSAA and the
packaged startup/gameplay test. Jason approved the final visual comparisons.

The music/effects balance comparison in **#4 remains in progress** and is not
changed by this release. Any further rank/damage discrepancy needs a specific
matched DOS encounter; no guessed global damage adjustment has been added.

Release installers, engine/editor packages and the Android APK are rebuilt by
the repository's release workflow. They contain no commercial Corridor 7 game
files; provide your own copy of the game. The self-contained local test package
is kept private and is not uploaded as a release artifact.
