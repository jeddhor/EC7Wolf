# EC7Wolf 1.0-beta299

EC7Wolf's beta number counts commits since milestone `20ee748`; it is separate
from its engine base, ECWolf 1.4.2-9-g1bff92d (18 February 2026), on upstream's
1.5.0pre line. This remains a prerelease beta.

## Original-game fidelity

- **Bonus floors give back what you carried in (#23).** Leaving a bonus floor,
  by its exit or by being killed there, restores what the original sets aside
  when you take the elevator: health, bullets, armor, visor charge,
  alien-weapon energy, and the weapons owned and in hand. Being killed on a
  bonus floor ends the floor, not the game, as in DOS. Ammo capacity and mines
  are not part of that set and keep what happened on the bonus floor; a live
  DOS run confirmed both. Score is kept.
- **Every floor change** now refills alien-weapon energy capacity and ends an
  Invulnerability Sphere, as the original's floor setup does. Both used to
  carry over.
- **The floor report pays its own bonus.** The status report's BONUS is the
  efficiency rating × 100; it had been paying the hit/miss accuracy award on
  every floor instead. Numbers, colors and drop shadows match DOS captures.
- **BONUS LEVEL HIT/MISS RATIO** is shown after a bonus floor: a block per
  shot fired and per shot hit, the accuracy, and its award, paid once. At the
  DOS run's 113 shots and 66 hits it matches the original pixel for pixel.
  Holding one key no longer skips both pages.
- **The Intruder Alert is a noise, not a summons (#24).** The wrong terminal
  raises the same one-frame noise as a gunshot. Aliens in connected rooms come
  after their usual delay; aliens behind closed doors hear nothing. Ambush
  aliens and bosses have a 25-in-256 chance per noisy frame of coming looking
  after half a second to two minutes. That ambush rule now applies to gunfire
  too, which ambushers used to ignore entirely.

## New setting

- **Options → Gameplay → Intruder Alert:** **Classic** (default) is the DOS
  behavior; **Map-wide** carries the alarm through every door and wall, as
  #24 asked, and changes nothing else. Like the shotgun setting, it applies to
  new games, is stored in saves and is chosen by the multiplayer host. Demos
  and older saves play Classic.

## Looks like a bug, is the original

With a few hundred shots fired on a bonus floor, the hit/miss page's blocks
push the accuracy, bonus and score lines off the bottom of the screen: the
score line starts to clip at about 640 shots at 50% accuracy. The original's
drawing code has no limit either, so the port draws it the same way. The
award is still paid. See "Not deviations" in `docs/corridor7.md`.

## Compatibility and validation

**Multiplayer peers must use the same beta:** the Intruder Alert rule raises
the network protocol to 11, so beta297 and beta299 cannot play together.
Saves from beta297 load. A beta297 save made *on* a bonus floor cannot know
what was carried in, so the inventory at load time becomes the starting point,
and the log says so. Existing save product-version compatibility is preserved.

The rules were read from the retail CD executable and checked against
controlled DOS runs: the bonus-floor restore and its exceptions in a live
debugger session, the report pages against DOS screenshots. The Intruder Alert
rule comes from the executable's code; a live DOS alarm comparison was not
run. New gates cover the restore, its exceptions, the lethal route, save/load
and all six bonus routes (`corridor7_bonus_floor`), and both alarm rules, the
ambush draw and the saved rule in a purpose-built map
(`corridor7_intruder_alert`). The full suite passes apart from one
environment-only failure (`xbrz_scaling`, a Pillow fault on the build
machine).

Not yet done: the original's end-of-game **MISSION HIT/MISS RATIO** page,
which belongs with the victory page that is still the port's own.

Release installers, engine/editor packages and the Android APK are rebuilt by
the repository's release workflow. They contain no commercial Corridor 7 game
files; provide your own copy of the game.
