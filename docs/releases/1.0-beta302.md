# EC7Wolf 1.0-beta302

EC7Wolf's beta number counts commits since milestone `20ee748`; it is separate
from its engine base, ECWolf 1.4.2-9-g1bff92d (18 February 2026), on upstream's
1.5.0pre line. This remains a prerelease beta.

## Combat matches the original (#25, #26, #27)

Major plays like the original again: aliens hit hard and take real effort to
kill. Both halves of combat were wrong, in opposite directions, ever since the
combat code was first written. The per-rank health change in beta299 was not
the cause.

- **Your weapons did four times the original's damage (#27).** Every damage
  roll in CORR7CD.EXE is 0-255; the port's were 0-1023. A point-blank hit is
  now 0-63, farther 0-42, with the shotgun adding 100, 50 or 25. A Major
  Animated Probe (150 health) takes about seven hits.
- **Weapons fire the way the original's attack table says.** The M-343 and
  assault cannon fire three rounds a cycle, one on each of three frames, and
  the dual blaster fires on both of its last two frames. The Taser is a
  short-range gun with no ammunition, not a melee punch.
- **Aliens did about a fifth of the original's damage (#26).** Their gunfire is
  Wolf3D's enemy shot: 0-63 beside you, 0-31 at two or three tiles, 0-15
  beyond. A Probe next to you now hits for 1-64 (DOS: "close to 0-65").
- **Every alien attack follows the original's state tables, shot for shot.**
  Tenaj, Rodex, Ttocs, Otrebor, Tymok and the Mechanoid fire the extra rounds
  the port was missing; Nerraw and Eniram shoot, and the Semaj bites, five
  times per attack; Ttocs has a 1-in-7 second shot. Solrac and Tebazile's
  forms fire hitscan rather than projectiles, and the alien plasma bolt does
  30-61. The Mechanoid fires with the Dual Blaster's sound.
- **The plasma rifle recharges at the original's pace (#25).** Alien-weapon
  charge refills by two per frame, not per tic: about a second and a half from
  empty to full. It keeps refilling after you switch to another weapon.

## The ending is the original's

- Leaving MAP40 plays `SEQFOUR`, MAP40's status report, then **MISSION HIT/MISS
  RATIO**: the bonus-floor page over the whole game's shooting, a block per
  hundred shots, with its own accuracy award. The port's own victory page is
  gone; the original has one in its executable that nothing ever calls.
- The high scores follow only if your score ranks. The table has the
  original's black drop shadows, the row you earn is a navy bar in a yellow
  frame, and it stays up for six seconds or a key, after dying as well.
- Every floor's shots and hits, bonus floors included, count toward the
  mission totals, which travel in saves.
- Holding a direction while a hit/miss page draws makes the blocks count up
  one at a time, as in DOS.

## Known differences

From the same examination of the original, not changed in this beta:
- **Pain into counterattack:** about a third of the hits on a Ttocs, Otrebor or
  Eitak send it straight into its attack.
- **Hit/miss accounting:** the hit/miss page counts a long-range miss as a hit.
- **Alien plasma splash:** alien plasma also damages actors near the player.
- **Damage-flash flicker:** the red damage flash flickers on a big hit,
  because the palette cycling resets the palette every frame.

See "Known deviations" in `docs/corridor7.md`.

## Compatibility and validation

**Multiplayer peers must use the same beta:** the combat rules raise the
network protocol to 12, so beta299 and beta302 cannot play together. Saves from
beta299 load; their mission totals start at zero, so the ending page counts
from the floor the save was made on, and the log says so. Aliens already
wounded in a save keep their health. Existing save product-version
compatibility is preserved.

The combat rules were read from the retail CD executable's code and state
tables (`docs/github-issue-verification-2026-11-15.md`) and measured in the
port. The ending was compared pixel for pixel with seeded DOSBox-X runs
(`docs/corridor7-ending-verification-2026-11-14.md`). A new gate,
`corridor7_ending`, covers the ending, and the definition checks now pin the
damage rolls and every alien attack sequence. Three gates whose fixtures
assumed the old damage were updated. One bot gate, `bot_mines`, fails on one
seed: a bot that mines a dead end walks back through its own mine as a last
resort. That route fallback predates this beta; the new damage only changes
the fight that leads up to it.

Release installers, engine/editor packages and the Android APK are rebuilt by
the repository's release workflow. They contain no commercial Corridor 7 game
files; provide your own copy of the game.
