# EC7Wolf 1.0-beta304

EC7Wolf's beta number counts commits since milestone `20ee748`; it is separate
from its engine base, ECWolf 1.4.2-9-g1bff92d (18 February 2026), on upstream's
1.5.0pre line. This remains a prerelease beta.

## Aliens shoot when their attack frame says so (#29)

In beta302 a Rodex could hit you on the very tic it decided to attack, before
it had drawn an attack frame and sometimes before it had turned to face you.
The combat rework in that beta copied each alien's attack sequence from the
original but ran every shot one frame early: the original fires a frame's
action as the frame *ends*, and the port fires it as the frame begins.

- Every alien attack now fires on the original's frames. A Rodex's first round
  lands six tics after it starts its attack, as its firing frame appears; when
  that round misses, the next comes thirty tics later.
- The same fix applies to every alien whose attack opens with its
  sound-and-shoot frame (Tenaj, Ttocs, Otrebor, the Mechanoid) and moves every
  later shot in every attack to its proper frame.
- Damage, accuracy and the number of shots are unchanged from beta302.

## Aliens face the way they are moving (#28)

Animated Probes, and every other rotating sprite, could look diagonal while
flying straight at you, worst toward the edges of a wide screen. The rotation
frame was chosen from the direction you were looking rather than the line from
your eye to the alien. Wolf3D's correction for that was written for 360-degree
angles and did nothing with ECWolf's finer ones.

- The frame now follows the actual line of sight, in both renderers, at every
  resolution and aspect ratio. A Probe flying at you is drawn face-on wherever
  it is on screen.
- This is drawing only; nothing in the simulation changed.
- Near the edges of a 4:3 view an alien can now show the neighboring frame
  where DOS did not. The original's approximation got about half the true
  angle; keeping it would bring this bug back on wide screens.

## Compatibility and validation

**Multiplayer peers must use the same beta:** shot timing raises the network
protocol to 13, so beta302 and beta304 cannot play together. Saves from beta302
load. Existing save product-version compatibility is preserved.

The attack timing was read from the retail CD executable's actor loop and state
tables, and the rotation fault from the engine
(`docs/github-issue-verification-2026-11-16.md`). Both were measured in the
port: a lab Rodex on Major went from 0 tics to its first hit on half of its
volleys to 6 or 36, and a Probe flying at a player turned 25 degrees away went
from rotation 1 (front-diagonal) to 0 (face-on). The definition checks now
forbid a shot on the first frame of any alien attack, and the actor trace
records each alien's facing. One bot gate, `bot_mines`, still fails on one seed
for the reason given in beta302's notes: a bot that mines a dead end can walk
back through its own mine as a last resort.

Release installers, engine/editor packages and the Android APK are rebuilt by
the repository's release workflow. They contain no commercial Corridor 7 game
files; provide your own copy of the game.
