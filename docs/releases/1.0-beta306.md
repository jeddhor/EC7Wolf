# EC7Wolf 1.0-beta306

EC7Wolf's beta number counts commits since milestone `20ee748`; it is separate
from its engine base, ECWolf 1.4.2-9-g1bff92d (18 February 2026), on upstream's
1.5.0pre line. This remains a prerelease beta.

## Shoot through the fences (#31)

Floor 11 could not be finished: its aliens stand behind electric fences, and
the port stopped every shot at them. In the original, shots and sight pass the
see-through screens marked on the map -- the electric fences, the girder
trusses and some glass -- for you and for the aliens alike.

- You can shoot through electric fences and girder trusses, and aliens behind
  them can see you and shoot back.
- Glass that stops shots in DOS still stops them, and so do closed force fields
  and doors.
- Plasma bolts still stop at a fence, as they do at any solid wall.

## The Adrenaline Boost raises your health maximum (#32)

- An Adrenaline Boost raises your maximum health by 300, up to 999, and then
  heals you up to the new maximum. Two in a row take you to 700.
- Health chambers, med bays and medic packs heal you up to that raised maximum,
  so they keep working above 100. Before, they refused at 100.
- Each new floor puts the maximum back to 100, as before.
- The pickup message now reads "Adrenaline Boost", as in the original.

## Alien weapons and charge packs work as in DOS (#34)

Picking up a Dual Blaster seemed to give nothing. In the original, an alien
weapon does not come with charge; it restores your energy *capacity*, which is
what firing wears down.

- Picking up an alien weapon (Dual Blaster, Plasma Rifle, Assault Cannon or
  Disintegrator) sets energy capacity back to 100, and a duplicate is always
  picked up. Charge then refills up to capacity on its own.
- An Alien Charge Pack adds 100 capacity, up to 999, and charge refills into it.
  At 999 the pack is left lying.
- Charge starts building as soon as you own the M-343, so your first alien
  weapon arrives charged.

## The Mechanoid sounds like itself (#33)

- The Mechanoid Warrior's footsteps, its occasional ambient sound and its death are its
  own sounds now; it was borrowing another alien's.
- You hear it only where you would in DOS: when its part of the floor is
  connected to yours through open doors, not across the whole floor.

## Med bays (#35)

- The med bay's blast shield plays its sound as it opens.
- The medic pack inside flashes the screen like any other pickup.

## xBRZ smoothing no longer freezes the picture (#30)

Every fixed xBRZ setting (2x to 6x) made the picture appear to freeze. At
Native render resolution a fixed factor enlarged each frame past the window and
shrank it back, so every frame cost far more than it could show.

- A fixed factor never enlarges past the window.
- Choosing a fixed factor while Render Resolution is Native also lowers Render
  Resolution to match (3x draws at a third of the window and enlarges it three
  times), which is what makes the setting visible.
- Render Resolution now offers 1/5 and 1/6, so every xBRZ factor has a match.

## Known differences

Found in the same examination of the original, not changed in this beta:

- **Charge pack weapon switch:** a charge pack in the original also switches
  back to your best alien weapon.
- **Duplicate human weapons:** the original takes a duplicate even when its
  ammunition is full; the port leaves it.
- **Health chamber pace:** the original's chamber heals a little each frame; the
  port's heals in one step.

See "Known deviations" in `docs/corridor7.md`.

## Compatibility and validation

**Multiplayer peers must use the same beta:** the line-of-fire, health and
pickup rules raise the network protocol to 14, so beta304 and beta306 cannot
play together. Saves from beta304 load. A boosted maximum is kept in saves made
from this beta on. Existing save product-version compatibility is preserved.

Each rule was read from the retail CD executable and measured in the port
(`docs/github-issue-verification-2026-11-17.md`). Two new gates cover the
changes: `corridor7_sight_screens` shoots through a fence in both directions
with a glass control, and `corridor7_health_energy` checks the health maximum,
charge and capacity through the ordinary pickup code. Two existing gates were
updated: `bot_perception` now treats the see-through screens as see-through,
and `multiplayer_commands` runs its duel longer, because since beta302's
damage changes the respawn it checks for comes after 400 tics. One bot gate,
`bot_mines`, still fails on one seed for the reason given in beta302's notes.

Release installers, engine/editor packages and the Android APK are rebuilt by
the repository's release workflow. They contain no commercial Corridor 7 game
files; provide your own copy of the game.
