# EC7Wolf 1.0-beta308

EC7Wolf's beta number counts commits since milestone `20ee748`; it is separate
from its engine base, ECWolf 1.4.2-9-g1bff92d (18 February 2026), on upstream's
1.5.0pre line. This remains a prerelease beta.

## Mines beep when they arm (#37)

In the original, a mine you drop beeps once you have stepped off it, to tell you
it is armed. The port's mines were silent.

- A dropped mine arms once you are about three quarters of a tile away, and
  beeps with the same sound the menus use. Only the player who dropped it hears
  the beep.
- An armed mine goes off for you or any alien within about six tenths of a tile,
  with the original's detonation sound. Before, both distances were half a tile.
- Mines can still be shot to set them off early.

## Armor stops at 999 (#38)

- Each Body Armor suit adds 200, and armor now stops at 999, as in the original.
  Before, three suits on floor 11 could take it past 1100.
- A suit is still picked up when you are at 999, as in DOS.

## Compatibility and validation

**Multiplayer peers must use the same beta:** the mine distances and the armor
cap raise the network protocol to 15, so beta306 and beta308 cannot play
together. Saves from beta306 load. Existing save product-version compatibility
is preserved.

Both rules were read from the retail CD executable
(`docs/github-issue-verification-2026-11-19.md`). `corridor7_health_energy` now
walks over six armor suits and checks 200, 400, 600, 800 and 999, with all six
picked up; the definition checks pin the mine distances, sounds and the armor
cap. The mine sounds themselves are not observable headlessly. A sanitizer
gate also caught an undefined-behaviour read in beta306's floor-start health
reset; it now reads the same value safely. One bot gate, `bot_mines`, still
fails on one seed for the reason given in beta302's notes.

Release installers, engine/editor packages and the Android APK are rebuilt by
the repository's release workflow. They contain no commercial Corridor 7 game
files; provide your own copy of the game.
