# EC7Wolf 1.0-beta310

EC7Wolf's beta number counts commits since milestone `20ee748`; it is separate
from its engine base, ECWolf 1.4.2-9-g1bff92d (18 February 2026), on upstream's
1.5.0pre line. This remains a prerelease beta.

## Charge packs are used up again (#39)

Since beta306, picking up a second Alien Charge Pack took your energy capacity,
and with it your charge, straight to 999, and the pack stayed on the floor.

- Each charge pack adds 100 capacity and is used up, as in the original. At 999
  capacity a pack is left lying for later, as before.
- The cause was in how the engine handles items that run an action when picked
  up: the first pack went into your inventory, and every later one was then
  refused *after* adding its 100. Left lying, it did it again every tic. Such
  items are now used up when picked up, as in ZDoom.

## Old saves get the new limits

A save remembers each item's limit, so saves made before beta308 kept the old
armor limit of 65535, and armor in them could still pass 999 (#38). Loading a
Corridor 7 save now applies the current limits: armor stops at 999 (armor
already above it comes down to 999), and alien energy capacity at 999.

## Compatibility and validation

**Multiplayer peers must use the same beta:** the pickup change raises the
network protocol to 16, so beta308 and beta310 cannot play together. Saves from
earlier betas load, with the limits above applied. Existing save
product-version compatibility is preserved.

`corridor7_health_energy` now lays ten charge packs in a row: capacity rises
100 at a time to 999, nine are taken, and the tenth stays; it fails on beta308.
The exit vortex and the other games' extra lives and quiz items use the same
pickup code; their gates pass, and a lab check confirms the vortex still ends
the floor. Details: `docs/github-issue-verification-2026-11-20.md`. One bot
gate, `bot_mines`, still fails on one seed for the reason given in beta302's
notes.

Release installers, engine/editor packages and the Android APK are rebuilt by
the repository's release workflow. They contain no commercial Corridor 7 game
files; provide your own copy of the game.
