# EC7Wolf 1.0-beta313

EC7Wolf's beta number counts commits since milestone `20ee748`; it is separate
from its engine base, ECWolf 1.4.2-9-g1bff92d (18 February 2026), on upstream's
1.5.0pre line. This remains a prerelease beta.

## Fixes

- **Mines** are dropped under you, as in the original, not in front of you
  (where they could end up inside a wall) (#42). Only projectiles set them
  off: the Plasma Rifle and alien shots trigger a mine, bullets and
  explosions do not, so mines no longer chain (#49).
- **Status bar card icons** colour-cycle again, as in the original (#44).
- **Dying with the visor on** no longer tints the death page, the high scores
  and the menus green or red (#41).
- **Saves** are listed in numeric order, so save 10 follows save 9 (#43).
- **Quick save** (F8) writes a slot of its own, listed as "Quicksave", without
  asking, and never touches your named saves. F9 loads it, still asking first;
  with no quicksave it opens the load menu (#45).
- **Duplicate weapons** are always picked up and top up your ammunition, even
  when it is full, as in the original (#48).
- **Floor 16's health chambers** work. The original treats every chamber panel
  as a chamber, with or without a door, and these two have none (#53).
- **The full automap on OpenGL** no longer shows the 3D view through the
  status bar's boxes at the full-screen view size (#52).

## New options

Gameplay options apply to new games, are decided by the host in multiplayer
and are kept in saves. Every one defaults to the original's behavior.

| Option | Choices | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gameplay > Alien Weapon Capacity | Classic: an alien weapon resets energy capacity to 100, as in the original. Keep Higher: it only raises a capacity below 100 (#40) | Classic |
| Gameplay > Original Map Errors | Classic, or Fixed: floor 17's secret pushwall slides far enough to reach the secret behind it (#51) | Classic |
| Gameplay > Confirm Quick Save | Ask before F8 overwrites the quicksave (#45) | Off |
| Graphics > Red Access Card Icon | The red card's status bar icon cycles blue in the original; this cycles it red, like the card itself (#46) | Off |
| Graphics > Fullbright Sprites | Draws sprites unshaded, as the original does (#47) | Off |

## Compatibility and validation

**Multiplayer peers must use the same beta:** the new rules and the mine,
weapon and chamber changes raise the network protocol to 17, so beta310 and
beta313 cannot play together. Saves from earlier betas load and use the
classic rules. Existing save product-version compatibility is preserved.

A new gate, `corridor7_ui_fixes`, checks the automap, the death page, the card
icons and quick save; `corridor7_health_energy` gains the capacity, mine,
chamber and floor 17 checks. Every one of these fails on beta310 where it can
run there. Details: `docs/github-issue-verification-2026-11-21.md`.

Release installers, engine/editor packages and the Android APK are rebuilt by
the repository's release workflow. They contain no commercial Corridor 7 game
files; provide your own copy of the game.
