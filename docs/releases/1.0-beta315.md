# EC7Wolf 1.0-beta315

EC7Wolf's beta number counts commits since milestone `20ee748`; it is separate
from its engine base, ECWolf 1.4.2-9-g1bff92d (18 February 2026), on upstream's
1.5.0pre line. This remains a prerelease beta.

## Bosses are heard only from your area (#50)

Tymok (floors 15 and 20), Solrac (floor 25) and the Eniram Boss could be heard
from anywhere on the floor while they chased you, so their voices played over
and over.

- In the original, an alien's ambient voice plays only when its area is joined
  to yours through open doors, and then only once in seven walk cycles. The
  port made the one-in-seven roll but never checked the area. Only the
  Mechanoid had been fixed, in beta304.
- Every Corridor 7 alien with an ambient voice now uses the area check: Tymok,
  Solrac, the Eniram Boss, the Mechanoid and Otrebor.

## Compatibility and validation

The change only affects which sounds you hear, so the network protocol stays
at 17: beta313 and beta315 can play together. Saves from earlier betas load.
Existing save product-version compatibility is preserved.

A new gate, `corridor7_boss_sounds`, has a Tymok and a Solrac chase the player.
They are heard while the player shares their area, and not at all once the
player is moved to a sealed one, though they keep chasing. It fails without
the fix. With the reporter's floor 15 save, Tymok was heard 75 times in a
minute from across the floor before the fix, and not at all after it.
Details: `docs/github-issue-verification-2026-11-21.md`.

Release installers, engine/editor packages and the Android APK are rebuilt by
the repository's release workflow. They contain no commercial Corridor 7 game
files; provide your own copy of the game.
