# EC7Wolf 1.0-beta317

EC7Wolf's beta number counts commits since milestone `20ee748`; it is separate
from its engine base, ECWolf 1.4.2-9-g1bff92d (18 February 2026), on upstream's
1.5.0pre line. This remains a prerelease beta.

## Save and load messages (#54)

The original reports "Game %s saved." and "Game %s retrieved." on the play
screen's message line. Quick save (F8), quick load (F9) and saving or loading
from the menus now do the same.

## Alien weapon charge at the elevator (#55)

Charge above 100 was emptied down to capacity at every elevator, and whenever
an alien weapon was picked up. In the original, a new floor resets capacity to
100 but leaves the charge alone, and the weapon recharges only while its charge
is below capacity. The port now does the same: 600 charge reaches the next
floor as 600.

## Mines hit as hard as the original's (#56)

The port exploded a mine once, with damage falling to nothing two tiles away,
so an alien walking onto one took about a third of what it takes in DOS. The
blast is now the original's:

- three hits, two tics apart, while the explosion's fourth picture shows;
- each roll is 100 to 610, halved per tile of distance, out to three tiles;
- the alien that set the mine off takes the full roll again on every hit.

Bots now treat a mine as dangerous out to three tiles.

## Force-field doors after a load (#58)

Saves did not record whether a force-field door was open. After a load, every
open door looked open and could be walked through, but stopped every shot in
both directions, and using it ran the opening sequence again. Saves now keep
the door's state, and saves from earlier betas are repaired when loaded.

## Compatibility and validation

**Multiplayer peers must use the same beta:** the charge and mine changes
raise the network protocol to 18, so beta315 and beta317 cannot play together.
Saves from earlier betas load. Existing save product-version compatibility is
preserved.

A new gate, `corridor7_saved_doors`, opens a force-field door, saves, loads
and fires through it. `corridor7_health_energy` gains the charge and mine
checks, and `corridor7_ui_fixes` the save message. Each fails on beta315.
#57 (infrared wall shading) is still open. Details:
`docs/github-issue-verification-2026-11-22.md`.

Release installers, engine/editor packages and the Android APK are rebuilt by
the repository's release workflow. They contain no commercial Corridor 7 game
files; provide your own copy of the game.
