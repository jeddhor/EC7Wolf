# EC7Wolf 1.0-beta319

EC7Wolf's beta number counts commits since milestone `20ee748`; it is separate
from its engine base, ECWolf 1.4.2-9-g1bff92d (18 February 2026), on upstream's
1.5.0pre line. This remains a prerelease beta.

## The original's shading (#57)

Corridor 7 does not light its world through a light model. It darkens each
wall column and sprite by a whole number of palette steps, chosen by distance,
and a pixel that reaches the bottom of its colour band turns black. The lamp
colours are never darkened. Its floor and ceiling are not textures but a fixed
screen pattern of colour pairs: gray over purple on most floors, red or green
on some floors between 20 and 29.

The port used ECWolf's smooth light model instead, which kept distant walls
much brighter. Under the infrared visor those bright colours turn red, which
is the shimmer #57 reported where the original shows black. The original's
rules and tables are now read from `CORR7CD.EXE` and reproduced in both the
software and OpenGL renderers. Against captures of the original in DOSBox-X,
floor 1's floor and ceiling are pixel-exact, and floor 26's walls are within a
few percent of the original's brightness in the normal view and under infrared.

**Options > Display** gains two settings:

- **Lighting:** Original (the default) or Enhanced, the port's previous
  lighting, which is smoother and brighter in the distance. Enhanced is
  unchanged, so it still shows the infrared shimmer.
- **Light Level:** 0 to 10, the brightness the original raises and lowers with
  its `=` and `-` keys.

## A full-height view

The status bar was one line too tall. It left a black row above the bar and
drew the weapon one row high. The view is now the original's full 160 lines,
and the weapon lines up with the original exactly.

## Compatibility and validation

Rendering only: the network protocol stays at 18, so beta317 and beta319 can
play together, and saves are unaffected.

A new gate, `corridor7_original_lighting`, checks floor 1's floor and ceiling
pixel by pixel and floor 26's wall brightness against the original, in both
renderers, and checks that the view reaches the status bar. Details:
`docs/github-issue-verification-2026-11-22.md`.

Release installers, engine/editor packages and the Android APK are rebuilt by
the repository's release workflow. They contain no commercial Corridor 7 game
files; provide your own copy of the game.
